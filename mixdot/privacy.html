Privacy Policy

Kurz auf Deutsch: Mixdot braucht kein Konto und hat keine Werbung, keine Analyse und kein Tracking. Die App sendet keine Daten an uns. Dein Spielstand bleibt auf deinem Gerät, Käufe wickelt Apple ab.

Effective date: November 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the iOS app Mixdot ("the App", "we", "us") handles data. Mixdot is developed by Mario Haesler, an independent developer based in Germany (see the Imprint for full contact details). In short: no account, no ads, no tracking, no analytics. The App does not send any data to us.

1. Data we collect

None. The App has no user accounts, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no crash reporting service, and we do not run a server for it. The daily puzzles are made on your device.

2. Data that stays on your device

Your progress (painted squares, times, hints, the recorded steps for the timelapse), your streak, your settings and whether Mixdot Plus is active are stored locally on your device only. They are removed when you delete the App.

3. Purchases

Mixdot Plus is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment and tells the App on your device which purchases are active; we never receive your name, payment details or Apple ID. Subscriptions are managed and cancelled in your App Store account settings. Apple's handling of data is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

4. Sharing your result

The Share button opens the iOS share sheet with a short text (puzzle number, time and a grid of coloured squares). Nothing is sent unless you choose an app or contact there yourself.

5. Children

The App collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.

7. Contact

Questions about privacy: mariohaesler@protonmail.com
