Mixdot

A daily colour logic puzzle

Paint every square red, yellow or blue. Each dot shows the mix of the squares it touches. One new puzzle every day for everyone, a little harder towards the weekend.

Mixdot Plus

The daily puzzle is always free. Mixdot Plus adds the whole archive, endless practice boards, statistics, frames and papers. To restore a purchase on a new device, open the palette button at the top right and tap Restore. Subscriptions are managed and cancelled in your App Store account settings.
